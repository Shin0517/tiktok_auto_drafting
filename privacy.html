Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Who We Are

Muson Video Scheduler is operated by Muson Studio for internal use only. This policy explains what data the Tool accesses when a TikTok account is connected.

2. Data We Access

With the account owner's authorization, the Tool uses the following TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic – the account's display name, avatar, and open ID, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • video.upload – used to upload videos to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

We do not access followers, messages, analytics, or any other account data.

3. Data We Store

We store the access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, along with the account's display name, in a private database used only by the Tool. We also keep a log of which videos were uploaded and when.

4. How Data Is Used

Stored data is used only to upload videos to the connected accounts. We do not sell, share, or disclose it to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Tokens are kept while the account remains connected. Account owners can revoke access at any time in TikTok's app settings, after which the token becomes invalid. To request deletion of all stored data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it within 7 days.

6. Security

Tokens are stored encrypted and are accessible only to authorized team members.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will always be posted at this address.
